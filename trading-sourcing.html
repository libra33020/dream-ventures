Four-Tier Quality Control & Inspection Milestones

Every sourcing engagement follows strict international inspection standards (ISO 2859-1 / ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) to ensure zero defect slippage before export containers depart.

IPC

Initial Production Check

Raw material validation, machine calibration, and first article verification before mass line start.

DUPRO

During Production Inspection

In-line tolerance checks at 20–50% completion to identify defect patterns and rectify early.

PSI

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Statistical AQL Level II sampling, cosmetic finish, functional testing, and packaging drop-tests.

CLC

Container Loading Check

Carton count validation, packaging integrity, pallet lashing, and official container seal verification.
